Hi {{firstName}}, a quick update on what changed at velai.

Save a search on velai.app and we'll email you when new matches come in, once a week. Stop anytime with one click.

You can also connect your own AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor) at velai.app/connect. It can search velai's jobs and read your résumé, so you can ask it about fit without leaving the chat.

A few other things we shipped: your résumé now has a Projects section, plus fields for GPA, honours, coursework, and activities under Education. Job pages now show similar open roles under "More like this," and that list no longer repeats an opening that's cross-posted elsewhere.

Fit scoring and tailoring run in your browser.

Go search: velai.app

Hi {{firstName}}, a quick update on what changed at velai.

Save a search on velai.app and we'll email you when new matches come in, once a week. Stop anytime with one click.

You can also connect your own AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor) at velai.app/connect. It can search velai's job data and check how a role fits you, without leaving the chat.

A few other things we shipped: your résumé now has a Projects section, plus fields for GPA, honours, coursework, and activities under Education. Job pages now show similar open roles under "More like this," and we stopped listing the same opening twice when it's cross-posted elsewhere.

Fit scoring and tailoring run in your browser.

Go search: velai.app

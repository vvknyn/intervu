Senior Platform Engineer

Buildkite · Remote · US · $180–220k
Live · no ghost signals No repost loop or 45+ day staleness on this listing.
About this role

About Buildkite

Buildkite helps engineering teams run fast, reliable CI/CD at scale. Our platform orchestrates pipelines across your own infrastructure so builds stay private, parallel, and under your control.

Role Overview

We're hiring a Senior Platform Engineer to own the systems that schedule, isolate, and observe customer workloads. You'll work across Go services, Kubernetes, and observability tooling with a small senior team.

What You'll Do

  • Design and ship durable scheduling / queueing components for multi-tenant CI
  • Improve isolation, cost controls, and failure recovery for agent fleets
  • Partner with Security on least-privilege defaults for customer-run agents
  • Instrument reliability SLOs and drive down noisy-neighbor incidents

You're a Strong Fit if You Have

  • 5+ years building production distributed systems
  • Deep experience with Kubernetes, Linux, and container runtimes
  • Comfort owning on-call for platform services
  • Clear written communication with customers and teammates

Nice to Have

  • Prior work on CI/CD, developer platforms, or build systems
  • Go or Rust in production
  • Experience with cost attribution for shared infra

Why You'll Love Working at Buildkite

  • Remote-first, async-friendly culture
  • Meaningful equity and competitive benefits
  • Customers who are themselves sophisticated engineering orgs
Compensation
Base $180,000–$220,000 USD · equity · benefits. Final offer depends on level and location.

About the role

Join our 24-bed Intensive Care Unit and provide direct patient care to critically ill adults.

  • Patient assessment, ventilator management and IV therapy
  • Medication administration and charting in Epic
  • Work with physicians, respiratory therapists and families
  • Triage of incoming critically ill patients from the emergency department

What you bring

Registered nurse licence with CNO, BLS and ACLS, 2+ years acute care experience, critical care or emergency experience preferred.

How it works

  • On job pages on sites such as LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever and Workday, a small Velai card shows your fit and what's known about the posting: whether it looks current, the pay, when it was posted. Each line says where it comes from.
  • On any other careers page, click the Velai button in your toolbar. To find it, click the puzzle piece next to the address bar and pin Velai.
  • From the card or the button, see the changes Velai proposes to your résumé for the job, accept the ones you want and get it as a one-page PDF.
  • On a job's application form, "Fill from your résumé" fills in your name, email, phone and links and attaches that PDF. You check it and send it yourself. Once the site confirms, the job goes into your Velai tracker.
